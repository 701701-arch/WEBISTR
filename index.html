          ELECTRO-ART DESIGN

          The physical twin of this dashboard

          This interface mirrors a working tabletop model — built from cardboard, foam board and reclaimed circuit boards, wired with Arduino-driven sensors, servo gates and conveyor motors. Old PCB fragments, resistors and capacitors are arranged as skyline silhouettes, turning the hidden machinery that keeps a city clean into something you can actually see.
